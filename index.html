    Case Study

    Handwriting Haptic Wristband

    Objective

    Convert pen error into real time haptic guidance.

    Role

    Hardware, firmware, web interface, and haptic patterns.

    Challenges

    Clear direction cues, low latency, and comfortable wear.

    Approach

    Map error vectors to motor sequences and iterate through prototypes.

    Iterations

    Iteration 1 — Passive Linkage

    Mechanical guidance to validate the core interaction.

    Iteration 2 — ERM Motors

    Vibration feedback enabled guidance, with limited directional clarity.

    Iteration 3 — Audio Exciter

    Higher amplitude vibration, still not direction selective.

    Iteration 4 — Multi LRA Wristband

    Improved directionality using four LRAs and timed sequences.

    Next Work

    Integrate enclosure and battery, then run user studies.

    System Direction

    On device processing and wireless data streaming.

    Wing Test

    Overview

    Built a cambered airfoil and stand, then measured lift using a load cell amplifier and microcontroller readout.

    Calibration

    Applied known weights, fit a load to voltage curve, and zeroed the system in tunnel for repeatable measurements.

    Design and Hardware

    Results

    Low Reynolds effects reduced lift growth and led to earlier stall, highlighting scale sensitivity in tunnel testing.
